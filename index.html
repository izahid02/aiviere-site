Questions

What is AIVIERE?

AIVIERE is a small-batch clothing label from Assam, India. We make heavyweight hoodies and track pants in limited, numbered editions, with artwork inspired by Assam's rivers, hills and people.

When does AIVIERE launch?

Our first collection, Drop 01, opens in January 2027 on aiviere.in.

What will Drop 01 include?

Drop 01 is five pieces: three heavyweight hoodies and two pairs of track pants, each made in a limited, numbered edition.

How do I get early access?

Join the waitlist on this page. Waitlist members get first access to Drop 01 before it opens to everyone, and a note when it goes live.

Who founded AIVIERE?

AIVIERE was founded in Assam by Mozahidul Islam (known as Zahid), founder, and Musta Hassan Billah, co-founder.

Where is AIVIERE from?

AIVIERE is from Assam, in northeast India. The Brahmaputra, the hills and the craft of the region shape every design.
